Interested in the Atlas of Climate Change in your own language? You can make it happen – and we'll help.

## Why adapt the Atlas?

The Atlas bridges the gap between dense scientific reports and the general public – and this format works. Adapting it for your language and country comes with real advantages:

- **Saves you hundreds of hours.** Our infographics and web platform are ready-made, so your team skips most of the data-cleaning, design, and development work from scratch.
- **The science is already checked.** Every core dataset has been reviewed by climate scientists, so you can trust the framework from day one.
- **It resonates locally.** Adding your own country's data – warming trends, emissions breakdown – turns abstract global numbers into something readers immediately recognise.
- **Publish under your own branding.** We're happy to simply be credited as the original author and data source.

## How we work together

The Atlas is published under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International Licence (CC BY 4.0), so the content is open. Because the graphics and platform are precise, we follow a simple, structured process of cooperation:

1. **Tell us you're interested** – write to us with your target language, region, and expected reach.
2. **A short call** – we'll talk through your team's capacity, distribution plans, and how costs might be covered.
3. **A brief agreement** – a short memorandum covering which pages change, how logos are used, and our final data check before launch.

Once we've agreed on the details, the practical work follows roughly three stages: translating the texts, adapting the infographics, and building the localised website. You can either do this yourselves (we'll give your team access to the templates and web platform, free of charge) or let us handle it for a transparent fee based on complexity.
